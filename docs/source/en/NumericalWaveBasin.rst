Numerical Wave Basin
========================================

:doc:`Marine Energy Structures <MarineEnergyStructures>` · :doc:`Research overview <Research>`

Wave generation, propagation and absorption, and interactions between the free surface and structures, determine the credibility of hydrodynamic computation. A numerical wave basin studies and validates these processes themselves, rather than simply supplying loads to a whole-system model. The focus is on identifying error sources and establishing a traceable, stepwise validation path.

- How can a finite domain generate target waves while controlling reflection and propagation error?
- How should free-surface, current and structure interactions be checked for mesh and time-step sensitivity?
- Can analytical, benchmark or experimental comparisons distinguish wave-generation error from response error?

Methods and contributions
--------------------------------------------------

Wave generation, propagation, absorption and free-surface modelling support interaction studies. Method credibility depends on boundary, mesh and benchmark validation; this page does not claim a separate demonstrated wave-basin result.

The validation sequence starts with wave generation and propagation, then examines structures and coupled cases. Conditions and errors need to be reported separately at each step.

Open questions
----------------------------------------

Wave conditions, boundary treatment and convergence checks need reproducible validation evidence. Current public material is insufficient to establish independent quantitative conclusions for this subdirection; no method breakthrough or all-condition validation is claimed.
