Join Us
========

Explore research with the group in structural wind engineering and marine energy structures, with AI-supported engineering methods. International Master’s and PhD applicants are welcome to discuss their interests. The group leader is Chao Li, Professor and PhD Supervisor.

Research fit
------------

Relevant preparation can include civil, structural, mechanical or ocean engineering, computational mechanics, applied mathematics or scientific computing. These are examples of connections to the research, not official programme entry requirements. Prior experience need not cover every method used in the group.

- **Structural Wind Engineering:** urban wind modelling, turbulent inflow, wind-induced structural response and graph-based response prediction.
- **Marine Energy Structures:** current work focuses on floating offshore wind — coupled system dynamics, data-driven design comparison, concrete floating structures and wave–structure modelling.

Read the :doc:`Research page <Research>` and one relevant highlight to identify a question you would like to explore.

Academic contact
----------------

Email `lichaosz@qq.com <mailto:lichaosz@qq.com>`_ with a brief introduction. For an initial academic conversation, the following are useful suggestions:

- a short CV covering your education and relevant coursework or experience;
- your intended degree and research interests;
- one or two questions connected to the group’s research, with an optional link to a report, code example or paper you are permitted to share.

These are suggestions for academic contact, not school admission requirements or a formal application checklist. A discussion helps explore research fit; it does not constitute an admission offer or a funding decision.
